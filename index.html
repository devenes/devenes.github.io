Enes Turan is a Platform Engineer and Google Developer Expert who focuses on demystifying large-scale cloud systems, Kubernetes infrastructure, and applied artificial intelligence systems.

Over the course of dozens of speaking sessions across international tech conferences, Google DevFests, Google I/O Extended events, and academic symposiums, Enes has engaged with thousands of engineers, architects, and developers across Europe, North Africa, the Middle East, and Central Asia.

His talks emphasize practical, production-proven architectures over transient hype: unpacking the inner workings of Kubernetes schedulers, running high-scale AI/ML workloads on GKE, implementing Site Reliability Engineering at scale, and architecting autonomous multi-agent workflows with the Google Agent Development Kit (ADK).
